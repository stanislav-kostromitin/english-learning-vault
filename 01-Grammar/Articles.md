# Articles

## Meaning
Use articles correctly in work English: a/an for singular countable nouns, the for specific things, and no article for general plural or uncountable nouns.

## Structure
- a bug
- an issue
- the fix
- no article in general statements

## QA Examples
- We found a bug in the build.
- The issue is in production.
- We need better reporting.

## Key Idea
Articles are often determined by whether the noun is specific or general.
