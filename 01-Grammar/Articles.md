# Articles

## Meaning

Articles show whether the listener should understand a noun as one new item, a specific known item, or a general idea.

## Structure

```text
a/an + singular countable noun: a bug, an issue
the + specific known noun: the bug, the issue, the fix
no article + plural/general/uncountable: bugs, logs, automation, reporting
```

## QA / Work Examples

- We found a bug in the build. New, non-specific bug.
- The bug is reproducible in staging. Now it is specific.
- The issue is in production. A known/specific issue.
- We need better reporting. General uncountable idea.
- Logs show several errors. General plural.

## Contrast

- I found a bug. First mention or one of many.
- I fixed the bug. The listener knows which bug.
- Automation is important. General concept, no article.
- The automation suite is failing. Specific suite, use `the`.

## Common Mistakes

- Wrong: I found bug.
- Better: I found a bug.
- Reason: `bug` is singular countable, so it needs an article.

- Wrong: The automation is important.
- Better: Automation is important.
- Reason: general uncountable concept usually uses no article.

## Practice

- Describe one new bug and then refer to the same bug again.
- Write one sentence with `automation` as a general concept.
- Write one sentence with `the` for a specific fix/deployment/report.

## Key Idea

Use `a/an` for one new countable thing, `the` for a specific known thing, and no article for general plural or uncountable ideas.
