# Comparative Adjectives

## Meaning

Use comparative adjectives to compare two things: quality, speed, stability, complexity, or priority.

## Structure

```text
short adjective + -er + than: faster than, smaller than
more + long adjective + than: more stable than, more complicated than
irregular: better, worse, farther/further
```

## QA / Work Examples

- This approach is faster than the previous one.
- The new test suite is more stable than the old one.
- This bug is worse than we expected.
- The staging environment is slower than production today.

## Contrast

- Comparative: This fix is safer than the workaround.
- Superlative: This is the safest option.

## Common Mistakes

- Wrong: This approach is more faster.
- Better: This approach is faster.
- Reason: do not use `more` with `-er`.

## Practice

- Compare two testing approaches.
- Compare staging and production.
- Compare two bugs by priority or impact.

## Key Idea

Use comparatives to show a difference between two things.
