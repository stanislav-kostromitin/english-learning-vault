## Core Principle

Do not choose English tenses by translating from Russian or by checking Jira task status. Choose the tense based on the meaning you want to communicate.

## Tense Choice Cheat Sheet

| Meaning | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| finished past time block | [[Past Simple]] | I worked on the feature from 9 to 12. |
| result important now | [[Present Perfect]] | I've fixed the bug. |
| activity/duration up to now | [[Present Perfect Continuous]] | I've been investigating the issue since morning. |
| current temporary action | [[Present Continuous]] | I'm checking the logs now. |
| fact/routine/stable situation | [[Present Simple]] | We run regression tests before release. |

## Important Comparisons

### worked on does not mean finished

- I worked on the feature from 9 to 12.
  - Meaning: I spent time on it.
  - We do not know whether it is completed.

- I finished the feature at 12.
  - Meaning: the feature is completed.

### result vs activity

- I've fixed the bug.
  - Focus: result.

- I've been fixing the bug since morning.
  - Focus: activity/duration.

### state verbs

- I understand the issue.
- I know the root cause.
- I believe the fix works.

Usually avoid: `I am understanding`, `I am knowing`.

## Personal Conclusions

- Do not choose the tense based on Jira status.
- Choose the tense based on what information the listener needs.
- A completed time block usually uses Past Simple.
- Current activity usually uses Present Continuous.
- Activity over a period up to now usually uses Present Perfect Continuous.
- Result now usually uses Present Perfect.
- Articles depend on whether the noun is new, specific, general, countable, or uncountable.
- Work prepositions are best learned as fixed phrases.
