# Passive Voice

## Meaning
Use passive voice when the focus is on the action, result, or the object of the action rather than the doer.

## Structure
- The bug was fixed yesterday.
- The deployment was delayed.
- The report is being reviewed.

## QA Examples
- The issue was reproduced in staging.
- The test suite was updated last night.
- The fix has already been verified.

## Key Idea
Passive voice is useful in QA reports and incident updates.
