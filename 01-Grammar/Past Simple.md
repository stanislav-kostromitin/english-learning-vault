# Past Simple

## Meaning

Use Past Simple for completed events or completed time blocks in the past.

## Structure

```text
V2 / did + verb
```

## QA Examples

- I fixed the bug yesterday.
- I worked on the feature from 9 to 12.
- I had two meetings.

## Key Idea

Past Simple does not always mean the whole task is finished. It can mean only that the time block is finished.

## Important Example

> I worked on the feature from 9 to 12.

Meaning:

- I spent time on the feature.
- We do not know whether the feature is completed.

> I finished the feature at 12.

Meaning:

- The feature is completed.
