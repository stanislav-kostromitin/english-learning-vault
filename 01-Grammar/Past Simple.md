# Past Simple

## Meaning

Use Past Simple for completed events or completed time blocks in the past. The whole task may still be open, but the time block is finished.

## Structure

```text
Positive: I worked / fixed / wrote
Negative: I did not work / fix / write
Question: Did you work / fix / write?
```

## QA / Work Examples

- I fixed the bug yesterday.
- I worked on the feature from 9 to 12.
- I had two meetings.
- We reviewed the logs after the deployment.

## Contrast

- I worked on the feature from 9 to 12. Meaning: I spent time on it; maybe it is not finished.
- I finished the feature at 12. Meaning: the feature is completed.
- I have fixed the bug. Focus: result now.
- I fixed the bug yesterday. Focus: finished past time.

## Common Mistakes

- Wrong: I have been writing tests from 9 to 11.
- Better: I wrote tests from 9 to 11.
- Reason: `from 9 to 11` is a completed time block.

## Practice

- Say three things you did yesterday at work.
- Write one stand-up sentence with a completed time block.
- Explain the difference between `worked on` and `finished`.

## Key Idea

Past Simple can describe a finished time block, not necessarily a finished Jira task.
