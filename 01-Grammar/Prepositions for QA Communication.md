# Prepositions for QA Communication

## Meaning
Prepositions matter a lot in QA and work English. Common patterns include in a meeting, on a call, at work, and work on a task.

## Structure
- in a meeting
- on a call
- at work
- work on a feature
- switch to a new branch

## QA Examples
- I am in a meeting.
- I am on a call with support.
- We are working on a new feature.

## Key Idea
Prepositions are often fixed in common work phrases.
