# Present Perfect vs Past Simple

## Meaning
Use Present Perfect for experiences or results connected to the present, and Past Simple for finished actions in a finished time period.

## Structure
- Present Perfect: I have fixed the issue.
- Past Simple: I fixed the issue yesterday.

## QA Examples
- I have already reviewed the logs.
- I reviewed the logs at 9 a.m.

## Key Idea
Use the time perspective to choose the tense.
