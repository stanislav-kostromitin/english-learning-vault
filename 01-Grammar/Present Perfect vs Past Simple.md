# Present Perfect vs Past Simple

## Meaning

Use Present Perfect when the result or experience matters now. Use Past Simple when you place the action in a finished past time.

## Structure

```text
Present Perfect: have / has + V3
Past Simple: V2 / did + verb
```

## QA / Work Examples

- I have already reviewed the logs. Focus: result now.
- I reviewed the logs at 9 a.m. Focus: finished time.
- We have deployed the fix. Focus: it is available now.
- We deployed the fix yesterday. Focus: when it happened.

## Contrast

The deciding factor is not Jira status. The deciding factor is the time perspective and what the listener needs to know.

## Common Mistakes

- Wrong: I have reviewed the logs at 9 a.m.
- Better: I reviewed the logs at 9 a.m.
- Reason: `at 9 a.m.` is a finished past time.

## Practice

- Make two sentences about the same bug: one with result now, one with exact past time.
- Explain why Jira status does not choose the tense.

## Key Idea

Present Perfect connects to now; Past Simple puts the action in finished past time.
