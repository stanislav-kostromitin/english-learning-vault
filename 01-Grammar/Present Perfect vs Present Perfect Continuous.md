# Present Perfect vs Present Perfect Continuous

## Meaning
Use Present Perfect for a completed result or experience. Use Present Perfect Continuous for activity, duration, or a recent visible process.

## Structure
- Present Perfect: I have written the report.
- Present Perfect Continuous: I have been writing the report for two hours.

## QA Examples
- I have fixed the bug.
- I have been fixing the bug since morning.

## Key Idea
Focus on result versus activity/duration.
