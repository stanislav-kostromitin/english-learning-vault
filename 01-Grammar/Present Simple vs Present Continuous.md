# Present Simple vs Present Continuous

## Meaning
Use Present Simple for routines and facts, and Present Continuous for temporary actions happening now or around now.

## Structure
- Present Simple: I work remotely every day.
- Present Continuous: I am working on a bug right now.

## QA Examples
- We usually review logs in the morning.
- We are reviewing logs because the build failed.

## Key Idea
The tense choice depends on whether the action is regular or temporary.
