# Present Simple vs Present Continuous

## Meaning

Use Present Simple for regular or stable situations. Use Present Continuous for temporary actions happening now or around now.

## Structure

```text
Present Simple: I work / she works
Present Continuous: I am working / she is working
```

## QA / Work Examples

- We usually review logs in the morning.
- We are reviewing logs because the build failed.
- The API returns 200 on success.
- The API is returning 500 errors today.

## Contrast

- I work on automation. Meaning: this is my role or regular work.
- I am working on automation. Meaning: this is my current task.
- We deploy every Friday. Meaning: schedule/routine.
- We are deploying a hotfix now. Meaning: current action.

## Common Mistakes

- Wrong: I am working in QA for five years.
- Better: I have been working in QA for five years.
- Reason: a duration up to now needs Present Perfect Continuous or Present Perfect.

## Practice

- Say one stable fact about your team.
- Say one current temporary task.
- Explain why each tense is used.

## Key Idea

Ask: is it generally true, or is it happening temporarily now?
