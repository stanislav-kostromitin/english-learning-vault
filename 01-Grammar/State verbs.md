# State verbs

## Meaning
State verbs describe states, feelings, beliefs, and possession rather than actions. They usually do not take the continuous form.

## Structure
- I know the answer.
- I understand the issue.
- I believe the fix works.

## QA Examples
- I know the root cause.
- I understand the current behavior.
- I like this approach.

## Key Idea
Use state verbs in simple forms unless you mean a temporary activity.
