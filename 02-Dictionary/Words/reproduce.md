# reproduce

**Translation:**
воспроизвести

**Meaning:**
To make a bug or problem happen again, usually to confirm and study it.

**QA / work example:**
- I can reproduce this bug.
- I couldn't reproduce the bug on staging.

**Related words:**
- [[bug]]
- [[investigate]]
- [[issue]]
