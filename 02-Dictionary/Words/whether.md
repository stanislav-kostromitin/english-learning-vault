# whether

Status: 🟨 Learning

## Translation
ли, независимо от того, будет ли

## Meaning
Used to introduce alternatives or to express doubt about two possibilities.

## Examples
- I need to know whether the fix works.
- We discussed whether to deploy today.
- Please confirm whether the issue is reproducible.
