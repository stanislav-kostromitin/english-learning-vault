# Mistake Journal

This file stores recurring mistakes and corrections.

## Grammar Mistakes

### 1. Using Present Perfect Continuous for a completed time block

❌ I have been writing automation tests from 9 a.m. to 11 a.m.

✅ I worked on automation tests from 9 a.m. to 11 a.m.

Reason: `from 9 to 11` is a completed time block.

---

### 2. Confusing task status with time perspective

❌ The task is not closed in Jira, so I cannot use Past Simple.

✅ I can use Past Simple if I describe a completed time block.

Example:

✅ I worked on the feature from 9 to 12, but it is still in progress.

---

### 3. Wrong verb form after have been

❌ I've been investigation this bug.

✅ I've been investigating this bug.

Reason: after `have/has been`, use `verb-ing`.

---

### 4. Wrong irregular verb form

❌ I have writed automation tests.

✅ I have written automation tests.

Forms:

```text
write → wrote → written
```

## Preposition Mistakes

### Meeting

❌ I'm on a meeting.

✅ I'm in a meeting.

### Call

✅ I'm on a call.

### Work

✅ I'm at work.

## Thinking Mistakes

### worked on ≠ finished

❌ I worked on the feature means I finished it.

✅ I worked on the feature only means I spent time on it.

### Present Perfect Continuous is not only for unfinished actions

❌ Present Perfect Continuous always means the action is not finished.

✅ Present Perfect Continuous emphasizes the activity or duration. The action may still be happening or may have just finished.
