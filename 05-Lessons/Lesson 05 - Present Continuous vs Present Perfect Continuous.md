# Lesson 05 - Present Continuous vs Present Perfect Continuous

## Lesson Goal

Learn to choose between:

- Present Continuous
- Present Perfect Continuous
- Present Perfect
- Past Simple

based on the meaning of the situation, not on direct translation from Russian.

## Main Idea

Do not choose the tense by the action itself.

Choose the tense by the perspective:

| Perspective | Tense | Example |
|---|---|---|
| What is happening right now? | Present Continuous | I'm working on a report. |
| What has been happening over a period until now? | Present Perfect Continuous | I've been working on a report all afternoon. |
| What result exists now? | Present Perfect | I've finished the report. |
| What happened in a completed time block? | Past Simple | I worked on the report from 9 to 11. |

## Photo vs Video Rule

### Present Continuous

Like a photo of the current moment.

> I'm investigating this issue.

Meaning: this is what I am doing now.

### Present Perfect Continuous

Like a video showing a period before now.

> I've been investigating this issue since 9 a.m.

Meaning: I started earlier and I am still doing it, or this activity explains the current situation.

## Important Examples

### Question: What are you doing?

Correct:

> I'm working on a report.

Why: the question asks about now.

### Question: What have you been doing this afternoon?

Correct:

> I've been working on a report.

Why: the question asks about activity over a period.

## Completed Time Block

If the time block is completed, use Past Simple.

Correct:

> I worked on automation tests from 9 a.m. to 11 a.m.

Not natural:

> I have been working on automation tests from 9 a.m. to 11 a.m.

## Daily Stand-up Example

> I worked on automation tests this morning. Then I had two meetings. After that, I investigated a bug, and now I'm fixing another one.

## Key Lesson Conclusion

- Present Continuous: now.
- Present Perfect Continuous: activity over a period until now.
- Present Perfect: result now.
- Past Simple: completed event or completed time block.

## Personal Notes

- Short interruptions do not usually stop Present Continuous.
- If I am writing a report and pause to answer a message, I can still say: I'm working on a report.
- `worked on` does not mean `finished`.
