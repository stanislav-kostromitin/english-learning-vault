## Course Method

The course should not be a mechanical list of tenses.

Main principle:

> Learn how native speakers choose grammar based on meaning, context, and what the listener needs to know.

Important example:

```text
I worked on the task from 9 to 11.
```

This does not mean:

```text
I finished the task.
```

It only means the speaker spent time on the task during a completed time block.
